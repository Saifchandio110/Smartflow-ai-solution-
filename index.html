FAQ

Common questions

What is AI automation?

AI automation uses software and AI tools to handle repetitive tasks, such as sorting messages, answering common questions, or moving information between apps, so people can spend time on higher-value work.

Which businesses can benefit from AI automation?

Small and medium-sized businesses, online stores, startups, agencies, and service providers often have routine tasks that can be simplified. If a task repeats often, it is worth discussing.

Can you build custom automation workflows?

Yes. We design workflows around your specific needs, subject to technical feasibility and the tools you use.

How much does an automation solution cost?

Cost depends on project scope, integrations, and requirements. Share your idea and we can discuss what is involved.

How long does a project take?
